# Senegal Market Context

Create the local context other marketing skills need before making recommendations for Senegal.

## Outcome

Add a `## Senegal Market Context` section to `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Preserve the rest of that document and its changelog.

Capture only facts supported by the user, the product, or current authoritative research:

- Priority geography: national, Dakar, Touba, another city, region, diaspora, or a combination
- Audience segments and purchasing roles
- Working languages: French, Wolof, or another named language
- Expected register: institutional, commercial, community, religious, technical, or conversational
- Price display and payment expectations, including whether amounts use FCFA/XOF
- Primary discovery and conversion channels
- Locally credible proof: field presence, customer references, partnerships, response time, address, or verified results
- Access constraints: device quality, bandwidth, literacy, assisted onboarding, and offline follow-up

## Rules

- Never treat Senegal as one homogeneous audience. Separate geography, language, age, profession, and purchasing power when they materially affect the plan.
- Do not translate French literally into Wolof. Ask for human validation of public-facing Wolof when nuance, religion, politics, law, health, or reputation matters.
- Do not invent official statistics, cultural claims, endorsements, testimonials, addresses, prices, or partner relationships.
- Verify current laws, platform availability, payment support, telecom conditions, and official statistics from primary sources before relying on them.
- Distinguish XOF from other currencies and state whether prices include taxes or fees when relevant.
- Treat WhatsApp, phone calls, social platforms, physical offices, agents, and websites as possible parts of one journey rather than assuming a website-only funnel.

## Handoff

After saving the context, use the relevant specialist skill:

- `senegal-local-seo` for local discovery
- `senegal-social-content` for social publishing
- `senegal-digital-campaigns` for paid acquisition
- `senegal-fcfa-pricing` for offers and monetization
- `senegal-whatsapp-commerce` for WhatsApp conversion flows
