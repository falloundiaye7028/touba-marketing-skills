# Senegal WhatsApp Commerce

Design an assisted conversion flow that remains clear, consent-based, and operationally realistic.

## Workflow

Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` and confirm the audience, offer, languages, operating hours, responsible team, expected volume, response-time target, escalation path, and system of record.

Map the journey:

1. Entry source and expectation set before the click
2. Welcome message and consent-aware next step
3. Minimal qualification questions
4. Product, service, appointment, membership, or support path
5. Price and payment explanation in FCFA/XOF where relevant
6. Human handoff and escalation
7. Confirmation, receipt or reference number, and follow-up
8. CRM, spreadsheet, or application status update

## Message Design

- Keep each message focused on one decision.
- Offer numbered choices when they reduce typing.
- Do not request sensitive personal or payment information unless necessary, authorized, secured, and compliant.
- State when a human will respond and what happens outside working hours.
- Write French and Wolof as separate reviewed variants when both are required.

## Guardrails

- Obtain appropriate consent before promotional follow-up and provide a simple opt-out.
- Never add contacts to lists, send bulk messages, or automate outreach without explicit authorization.
- Verify current WhatsApp Business and template-message rules before implementation.
- Do not treat chat history as the only customer record when fulfillment, money, membership, or complaints are involved.

## Deliverable

Return the flow, message templates, qualification fields, ownership rules, status model, response-time target, opt-out language, measurement plan, and implementation dependencies.
