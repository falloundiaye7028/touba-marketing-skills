# Senegal FCFA Pricing

Create understandable, sustainable pricing based on evidence rather than assumed purchasing power.

## Context and Research

Read `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Identify the buyer, payer, use frequency, alternative cost, sales channel, servicing cost, taxes or fees, and whether payment is individual, household, business, association, school, or institution.

Use current primary sources when laws, taxes, regulated fees, payment limits, or official economic data affect the recommendation.

## Pricing Work

- Display money consistently as `X FCFA` or `X XOF` according to the brand convention.
- State billing period, included usage, taxes, transaction fees, setup fees, renewal, cancellation, and refund terms.
- Test packaging by value and customer segment before using discounts.
- Consider installment or milestone payments only when operations can support collection and reconciliation.
- Distinguish list price, negotiated enterprise quote, promotional price, and subsidized access.
- Model gross margin, collection risk, support cost, and payment failure—not only willingness to pay.

## Guardrails

- Do not infer affordability from nationality or location alone.
- Do not invent competitor prices, official price indices, taxes, or mobile-money fees.
- Avoid deceptive crossed-out prices, hidden fees, forced urgency, and unclear automatic renewal.
- Pair with the repository's `pricing`, `offers`, and `paywalls` skills when deeper packaging or conversion work is required.

## Deliverable

Provide assumptions, recommended packages, FCFA price points or research ranges, value metric, payment cadence, risks, and a validation plan. Label estimates clearly.
