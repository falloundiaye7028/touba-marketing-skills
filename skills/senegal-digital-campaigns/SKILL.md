# Senegal Digital Campaigns

Design measurable campaigns adapted to the user's actual market, sales capacity, and conversion path.

## Inputs

Read `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Confirm objective, offer, geography, audience, languages, budget in FCFA/XOF, conversion event, destination, follow-up owner, and response-time capacity.

## Plan

1. Choose one primary outcome: awareness, qualified conversation, lead, sale, registration, donation, membership, or event attendance.
2. Match the platform and destination to observed audience behavior. Consider website, landing page, native form, phone, or click-to-WhatsApp.
3. Separate Senegal-resident and diaspora audiences when economics, message, or timing differ.
4. Build creative variants around distinct problems, proof, offers, or formats.
5. Define tracking before launch: UTMs, platform event, CRM or sheet capture, lead status, and offline outcome when applicable.
6. Set a learning budget and decision cadence appropriate to expected conversion volume; do not promise a universal cost per result.

## Measurement

Report spend and revenue in FCFA/XOF. Separate platform-reported results from verified business outcomes. Track response time, qualified rate, show-up rate, close rate, cost per qualified lead, and collected revenue when relevant.

## Guardrails

- Verify current platform policies, targeting availability, political-ad rules, taxes, and payment options before launch.
- Never invent benchmarks or promise results.
- Do not publish, fund, pause, or materially change a live campaign without explicit authorization at action time.
- Use the repository's `ads`, `ad-creative`, `analytics`, and `attribution` skills for deeper platform or measurement work.
