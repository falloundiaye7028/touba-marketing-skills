# Senegal Social Content

Create platform-native content grounded in the product's Senegal market context.

## Before Writing

Read `.agents/product-marketing.md`, especially `## Senegal Market Context`. If it is missing, use `senegal-market-context` first or clearly label assumptions.

Confirm the objective, audience, platform, language, call to action, publication timing, and whether the content represents a brand, media outlet, association, public figure, or political organization.

## Content Standard

- Lead with one clear idea and one action.
- Write natural French appropriate to the audience; avoid imported marketing jargon when a simple phrase works.
- Use Wolof only at the requested level. Preserve names, slogans, religious language, and official wording exactly when supplied.
- For bilingual content, write each language as a complete message instead of alternating every sentence.
- Make visuals readable on mobile and keep essential text away from platform interface zones.
- Separate an organic post, ad copy, video script, caption, and community reply; do not present one asset as universally reusable.
- Provide a fact-check note for claims involving money, statistics, institutions, public policy, elections, health, or breaking news.

## Editorial Safety

- Never fabricate quotations, testimonies, crowds, endorsements, results, or event attendance.
- Label sponsored or paid partnerships clearly.
- For political or civic content, distinguish verified information, opinion, campaign messaging, and calls to action. Check current election and advertising rules before publishing.
- For news content, preserve source attribution and separate reporting from commentary.
- Require human review for sensitive Wolof, religious references, political persuasion, crisis communication, and accusations.

## Deliverable

Return platform, audience, objective, final copy, visual direction, CTA, suggested publication window, and verification items. When requested, include 3 variants that change the hook rather than merely changing adjectives.
