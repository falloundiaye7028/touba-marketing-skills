# Senegal Local SEO

Improve discovery without creating thin city pages or inventing local presence.

## Context

Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` and use `senegal-market-context` when local context is missing. Pair with the repository's `seo-audit`, `site-architecture`, and `schema` skills when their broader workflows apply.

## Audit

Check:

- Search intent by service, problem, city, region, and language
- Crawlability, indexability, canonical URLs, sitemap, metadata, and internal linking
- Consistent business name, address, phone, hours, and service area wherever published
- Google Business Profile completeness and ownership when a physical or service-area presence is real
- Local landing pages with unique evidence, services, logistics, FAQs, and contact paths
- Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, Article, NewsArticle, Event, or other appropriate structured data
- Mobile speed and tap-to-call or WhatsApp usability
- Reputation signals and a compliant process for requesting genuine reviews
- French/Wolof keyword differences based on observed queries rather than literal translation

## Guardrails

- Never fabricate locations, branches, reviews, coordinates, ratings, or local customers.
- Do not mass-produce near-duplicate pages for every commune or region.
- Verify addresses and service areas with the user before publication.
- Use primary sources for administrative geography and current official information.
- Report unknowns as unknowns; lack of tool access is not an SEO failure.

## Deliverable

Prioritize findings by impact, evidence, effort, and dependency. For every proposed location page, state the distinct user value that justifies its existence.
